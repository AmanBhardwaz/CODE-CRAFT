REAL STORY

LOVE FOR ANIMAL

Niki and her mother returned home from the market. Niki loved animals very much, but her mother did not share the same affection for them. As they were walking back, Niki spotted a sad and dirty cat by the side of the road. Moved by the cat's condition, Niki wanted to help, but her mother was initially dismissive, not wanting to deal with an animal. Despite her mother's initial refusal, Niki persisted, pleading with her to help the cat. After some thought, Niki's mother remembered that she could ask for help through an app. She assured Niki that she would use the app to find someone who could care for the cat. This made Niki very happy. Niki's mother quickly registered on the app and shared their location to get assistance. Soon, help arrived, and the once-sad cat was now looking much happier. Niki was overjoyed to see the cat so well-cared for, and both she and the cat were happy. .

HOW OUR WEBSITE HELP PEOPLE

One day, there was a boy named Jai who was on his way to college with his friends. As they were walking, Jai noticed a cow on the side of the road. The cow looked sick and neglected, and Jai suspected that someone might have stolen the cow and abandoned it due to its poor health. Instead of continuing on to college, Jai decided to help the cow. He registered on an app, contacted the helpline, and shared his location to get assistance for the cow. Thanks to his quick actions, the cow received the help it needed, and today, the cow's health has completely improved
